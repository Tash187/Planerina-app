Delete Your Account

Last Updated: March 2026

How to Request Account Deletion

If you would like to delete your Planerina™ account and all associated data, please send an email to:

support@planerina.app

Please include the following in your email:

  • Subject line: "Delete Account Request"
  • The email address associated with your Planerina™ account
  • Your name (optional, but helps us locate your account)

What Happens When Your Account Is Deleted

Once we process your request, the following data will be permanently deleted:

  • Your account information (email, password, account ID)
  • Wedding details (couple names, date, location, theme)
  • Budget and expense information
  • Guest list and RSVP information
  • Vendor details and communications
  • Timeline and task information
  • AI Assistant conversation history
  • App usage data and preferences

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Please make sure you have saved any information you need before requesting deletion.

Processing Time

We will process your deletion request within 30 days of receiving your email. You will receive a confirmation email once your account and data have been deleted.

Active Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your subscription. If you have an active subscription, please cancel it through your device's app store settings (Apple App Store or Google Play Store) before requesting account deletion to avoid further charges.

Questions?

If you have any questions about the account deletion process, please contact us at: weddings@planerina.app
